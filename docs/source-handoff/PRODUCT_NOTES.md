# Product notes

## Corporate positioning reminder

Revisit this when designing the corporate product, enterprise sales materials, or IT deployment:

- The Lissa / Anya / Alec family is warm and memorable, but corporate users may prefer a nearly generic utility that fades into the background.
- Keep Alec quiet, neutral, and operational. Emphasize reliability, automation, governance, and low cognitive load over personality.
- The individual Anya experience can retain more warmth and visible character.
